Understanding Free Fire Sensitivity Settings

Sensitivity is an important part of aiming and camera control. Different categories influence different types of screen movement.

Setting Purpose
General Controls normal camera movement and aiming.
Red Dot Affects aiming through the Red Dot sight.
2X Scope Helps control aiming at medium distances.
4X Scope Influences aiming through the 4X Scope.
Sniper Scope Affects precision with sniper weapons.
Free Look Determines how quickly players can look around.

There is no universal sensitivity combination for every player. Screen size, touch response, finger movement, and personal experience all influence the preferred values.

Tips for Choosing a Comfortable HUD Layout

A good HUD arrangement should make important actions easy to perform during matches.

Keep your fire button within comfortable reach and choose a suitable button size. Place movement controls where your fingers can access them naturally. Make sure jump, crouch, and scope buttons do not interfere with each other.

You can also test different layouts in Training Mode. Try small adjustments and observe how they affect your movement and aiming. This approach helps you understand your control preferences without changing everything at once.
